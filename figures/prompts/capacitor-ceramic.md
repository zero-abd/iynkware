# Fig. P33 — Ceramic capacitor (Colorfigures prompt)

Make a colourful, hand-inked isometric figure of two **radial ceramic capacitors** (100 nF and 10 uF) standing in a
white mini breadboard on a pale green cutting mat, with a flat blue cast shadow. Yellow-brown dipped body about 5 x
2.5 mm and 5 mm tall on two 5.08 mm legs, the three digit code ("104", "106") printed on top.

- **Value ▾** (100 nF, 1 uF, 10 uF) changes the code and the label; **Show ▾** puts it over its voltage (burnt, with smoke).
- Not polarised. Open at DC. **See-through** clears the body to the stack of interleaved electrode plates.
  Label: Electrode plates.

Look: Colorfigures ink and paper, flat banded shading. Facts: docs/facts/analog-parts.md section 4.
