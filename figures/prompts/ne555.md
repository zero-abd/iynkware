# Fig. P32 — NE555 timer (Colorfigures prompt)

Make a colourful, hand-inked isometric figure of an **NE555 timer** in an 8-pin DIP straddling the centre channel of
a white mini breadboard on a pale green cutting mat, with a flat blue cast shadow. Black moulded body 9.8 mm long and
6.35 mm across, a notch and a dot at pin 1, "NE555" printed on top, eight tinned legs bent down into the holes.

What it does:

- **Show ▾** (part page): off, output high, output low, burnt. The label reads "U1 · output high" or "U1 · burnt".
- **See-through** clears the body to show the lead-frame fingers, the silicon die (it glows while the output is high)
  and the gold bond wires. Labels: Silicon die, Bond wires.
- Pins: 1 GND, 2 TRIG, 3 OUT, 4 RESET, 5 CONT, 6 THRES, 7 DISCH, 8 VCC. Supply 4.5 V to 16 V; the comparators trip at
  one third and two thirds of the supply.

Look: Colorfigures ink and paper, flat banded shading. Facts: docs/facts/analog-parts.md section 2 (Texas Instruments SLFS022).
