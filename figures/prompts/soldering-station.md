# Fig. L6 — Soldering station (Colorfigures prompt)

Make a colourful, hand-inked isometric figure of a **hobby soldering station** on the pale green cutting mat with a
flat blue cast shadow: a dark control box 80 mm wide, 140 mm deep and 95 mm tall with a blue front ("SOLDERING
STATION", a black temperature dial with a white pointer over a 200-480 °C scale, a red HEAT lamp, a red power rocker
and the iron's socket), and beside it a round black stand with a cone-shaped steel spring that holds the iron and a
tray with a yellow cellulose sponge. The iron has a thin steel tip, a steel barrel and a blue grip, and its black
cable runs over the mat to the socket.

What it does:

- **Power on/off**, **Pick up / put back** (the iron moves between the spring and a hand-held pose over the front of
  the mat), **Temperature ▾** (200 to 450 °C; pressing the dial steps it), **Sponge ▾** (damp, dry).
- The tip warms toward the dial in about 20 s and cools slowly; the HEAT lamp shows while it heats. A hot tip does not
  glow: only a faint shimmer rises from it.
- The tutor hears `on`, `tempSet`, `inHolder`, `sponge`, `tipC`, `heating` and `mistake`: `iron-left-out` (out of the
  stand over 15 s while on), `too-hot` (400 °C or more), `dry-sponge`.

Look: Colorfigures ink and paper, flat bands, the spring as a fine light coil with an ink edge, heavy silhouette on
the box.
