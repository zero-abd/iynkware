# Fig. L2 — 250 mL conical flask (Colorfigures prompt)

Make a colourful, hand-inked isometric figure of a **250 mL narrow-neck Erlenmeyer flask** in clear borosilicate
glass on the pale green cutting mat with a flat blue cast shadow: an 85 mm base, straight sloping sides up to a 34 mm
neck with a beaded rim, 145 mm tall (ISO 1773). White graduations every 50 mL from 50 to 250 on the front, "250 mL"
printed near the foot. The glass is a faint tint with an ink outline and a white highlight streak; the liquid inside
shows through it.

What it does:

- **Liquid ▾** (the same five as the beaker) and **Volume ▾** (0 to 300 mL); the level is computed from the cone's
  inside, so the marks and the liquid agree.
- **Fill 50 mL**, **Empty**, and **Swirl**: for three seconds the surface tips about 12° and its high side runs
  round the flask, the way a wrist swirl moves it.
- The tutor hears `mistake`: `swirl-too-full` (swirling with more than 150 mL), `overfilled` (past 250).
- See-through labels the narrow neck, the sloping sides and the liquid level.

Look: Colorfigures ink and paper, nearly invisible glass, the liquid in flat bands, heavy silhouette.
