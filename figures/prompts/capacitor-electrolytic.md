# Fig. P34 — Electrolytic capacitor (Colorfigures prompt)

Make a colourful, hand-inked isometric figure of two **radial aluminium electrolytic capacitors** (100 uF 25 V and
1000 uF 16 V) standing in a white mini breadboard on a pale green cutting mat, with a flat blue cast shadow. Blue
sleeve, a pale stripe down the negative side, the value printed on the top, two legs (the plus leg longer).

- **Capacitance ▾**, **Rated voltage ▾** and **Show ▾** (ok, put in backwards, over its voltage). A reversed or
  over-voltage part vents: the top splits and smoke rises. The label reads "C1 · 100 µF 25 V" or "C1 · vented".
- **See-through** clears the sleeve to the wound foil, the rubber seal and the two lead tabs.

Look: Colorfigures ink and paper, flat banded shading. Facts: docs/facts/analog-parts.md section 4.
