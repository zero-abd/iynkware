# Fig. L9 — Function generator (Colorfigures prompt)

Make a colourful, hand-inked isometric figure of a **benchtop DDS function generator** (220 mm wide, 90 mm tall,
200 mm deep) on the pale green cutting mat with a flat blue cast shadow: a light case with vents and a slate blue
front panel: "FUNCTION GENERATOR 0.1Hz-1MHz", a backlit yellow-green LCD (the frequency big, the waveform, amplitude
and OUT ON/OFF small), three grey waveform keys with sine, square and triangle symbols (the chosen one lit amber), a
big FREQUENCY knob and an AMPLITUDE knob, an OUTPUT key with a green lamp, the BNC "OUT 50Ω" socket and a green
POWER key. A black BNC lead runs from OUT over the mat to its load: a BNC plug "to the scope", or a splitter to red
and black crocodile clips with a 1 kΩ resistor between them, the clips touching (a short) or apart.

What it does:

- **Output on/off**, **Power on/off**, **Waveform ▾**, **Frequency ▾** (1 Hz to 1 MHz), **Amplitude ▾** (0.1 to 20 V
  peak-to-peak), **Load ▾**; the knobs and the waveform keys can be pressed too.
- It is a 50 Ω source: the voltage at the load is the setting × R / (R + 50).
- The tutor hears the settings, `vLoadPp`, `iPeakmA`, `signal` (what the oscilloscope takes as its demo signal) and
  `mistake`: `output-shorted`, `overdrive` (20 V into the scope).

Look: Colorfigures ink and paper, flat bands, a glowing LCD and lamp, heavy silhouette on the case.
