# Fig. L11 — Digital balance (Colorfigures prompt)

Make a colourful, hand-inked isometric figure of a **0.01 g top-pan balance** (200 mm wide, 230 mm deep, 60 mm
tall) on the pale green cutting mat with a flat blue cast shadow: a white body with a sloped front carrying a
yellow-green LCD (the reading in grams, "NET" once tared), a grey ON/OFF key and a blue TARE key, and a 120 mm
stainless pan on top. On the pan: nothing, a translucent white weighing boat (89 mm square), or the boat with a
small white heap of sodium chloride.

What it does:

- **Tare (zero)**, **Power on/off**, **On the pan ▾** (nothing, the empty boat, the boat with the sample),
  **Sample ▾** (2.50, 5.00, 10.00 g); pressing TARE or ON/OFF works too.
- The reading is the load minus the tare.
- The tutor hears `pan`, `sampleG`, `tareG`, `on`, `readingG`, `massG`, `net` and `mistake`: `no-tare` (the sample
  weighed without taring the empty boat), `tared-with-sample` (TARE pressed with the sample in).

Look: Colorfigures ink and paper, flat bands, a glowing LCD, a bright steel pan.
