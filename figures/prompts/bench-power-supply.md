# Fig. L4 — Bench power supply (Colorfigures prompt)

Make a colourful, hand-inked isometric figure of a **single-channel 0-30 V, 0-5 A linear bench power supply** on the
pale green cutting mat with a flat blue cast shadow: a light grey steel case 150 mm wide, 125 mm tall and 240 mm deep
with vent slots on top, and a dark front panel: two 4-digit seven-segment readouts at the top (volts in red, amps in
green, the unlit segments faintly visible), "DC POWER SUPPLY 0-30V 0-5A" above them, a VOLTAGE and a CURRENT knob
with white pointers, a green CV and a red CC lamp between them, a red rocker POWER switch bottom left and three 4 mm
binding posts bottom right: - (black), GND (green) and + (red). On the mat in front, clipped to + and - by a red and
a black lead: a 100 Ω 2 W resistor, a 10 Ω 5 W cement resistor or a wire link.

What it does (the real behaviour of a lab supply):

- **Power on/off** (or the rocker), **Voltage ▾**, **Current limit ▾**, **Load ▾**; pressing a knob steps it.
- It holds the set voltage (CV lamp) until the load would draw more than the limit; then it holds the current at the
  limit and the voltage falls (CC lamp). The readouts show the output, not the settings. A resistor asked to burn
  more than its rating glows orange and smokes.
- The tutor hears `on`, `vSet`, `iSet`, `load`, `vOut`, `iOut`, `mode` (CV, CC), `pLoadW`, `loadHot` and
  `mistake`: `short-high-limit`, `resistor-overload`, `limit-at-max`.
- See-through fades the case to show the mains transformer, the pass transistor on its heatsink, the control board
  and the fan; **Exploded view** lifts the cover off.

Look: Colorfigures ink and paper, flat bands, glowing readouts and lamps, heavy silhouette on the case.
