# Fig. L5 — Simple pendulum (Colorfigures prompt)

Make a colourful, hand-inked isometric figure of a **simple pendulum on a retort stand** on the pale green cutting
mat with a flat blue cast shadow: a slate-blue cast-iron base 200 x 130 x 12 mm, a 12 mm steel rod 600 mm tall at
one end, a boss head with a black thumbscrew 560 mm up holding a steel clamp rod out over the base, and from its end a
thin cream string with a 23 mm bob (brass or wood) and a small steel eye on top.

What it does:

- **Release / stop** (or press the bob) swings it across the clamp rod on the figure's clock with its real period:
  T0 = 2π√(L/g) for small swings, longer for big ones (T ≈ T0 (1 + θ0²/16 + 11θ0⁴/3072)).
- **String length ▾** (200 to 500 mm, pivot to the centre of the bob), **Release angle ▾** (5°, 10°, 20°, 45°) and
  **Bob ▾** (brass 50 g, wood 5 g: the period does not change, which is the point).
- The stats show the period, the swings counted and the small-swing formula. The tutor hears `swinging`, `swings`,
  `periodS`, `periodSmallS`, `bobGrams` and `mistake`: `large-angle` (released from more than 15°).
- See-through draws the arc of the swing dashed and the length L in red, from the pivot to the bob's centre.

Look: Colorfigures ink and paper, flat bands, thin steel rods with light silhouettes, a heavy silhouette on the base.
