# Fig. P35 — Inductor (Colorfigures prompt)

Make a colourful, hand-inked isometric figure of two **axial moulded inductors** (100 uH and 10 mH) lying in a white
mini breadboard on a pale green cutting mat, with a flat blue cast shadow. A pale green body about 7 mm long and 3.4 mm
across with three colour bands read as microhenries (brown black brown, brown black orange).

- **Value ▾** (100 uH, 10 mH) recolours the bands; **Show ▾** puts it over its current (burnt, with smoke).
- **See-through** clears the body to the ferrite core and the copper winding. Labels: Winding, Ferrite core.

Look: Colorfigures ink and paper, flat banded shading. Facts: docs/facts/analog-parts.md section 5.
